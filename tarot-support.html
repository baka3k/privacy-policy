Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open Mystic Tarot, go to the Paywall / Subscription screen or Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed into the same Apple ID (iOS) or Google Account (Android) used for the original purchase.

How do Free Tier, Tarot Basic, and Tarot Pro quotas work?

New installations receive 5 free lifetime trial reading units (no monthly reset). Once exhausted, you can subscribe to Tarot Basic (150 reading units/month) or Tarot Pro (400 reading units/month). Each single-card AI interpretation or holistic synthesis counts as 1 reading unit.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

iOS: Open Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > Mystic Tarot.
Android: Open Google Play Store > Profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Mystic Tarot.

How do I delete my reading history?

Your Tarot reading history is stored locally on your device. You can delete individual or all readings directly inside the app, or remove all local data by uninstalling the application. For server-side quota record inquiries, email baka3k@gmail.com.

How do I request a refund?

Because payments are processed directly by Apple and Google, developers cannot issue refunds directly. For iOS purchases, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Android purchases, use your Google Play Store order history.
