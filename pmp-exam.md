# Privacy Policy, Terms of Use & Customer Support

**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Application:** PMP Exam Prep: Practice Tests (iOS & Android)  
**Developer:** Baka Team

Welcome to **PMP Exam Prep: Practice Tests**. This document outlines our data privacy practices, terms of service, and official support channels.

---

## 1. Privacy Policy

**PMP Exam Prep is an offline-first educational application.**  
We are firmly committed to user privacy: **we do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal user data.**

### 1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or location data.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in.
- **Zero Third-Party Ads or Trackers:** The App contains no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app identifiers.
- **No Biometric Data:** The App does not access device sensors, microphones, or cameras.

### 1.2 On-Device Data Storage
All question attempts, mock exam scores, bookmark lists, and spaced-repetition statistics are stored strictly on your local device using an encrypted local database (SQLCipher / SQLite). Deleting the application removes your local database history unless backed up via native device OS backups (e.g., Apple iCloud or Google Drive).

### 1.3 Platform Processing
Purchases are processed securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not collect, view, or retain your payment details. Anonymous, aggregated diagnostic and crash reports may be provided by Apple TestFlight or Google Play Console to ensure technical stability.
