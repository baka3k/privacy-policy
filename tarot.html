Part 1: Privacy Policy

1.1 Core Principles: Guest-First & Privacy by Design

Privacy by Design: Mystic Tarot does not require account creation, logins, or personal profile data. All reading histories remain strictly stored on your own device.
  • No Account Required: You can explore Tarot spreads immediately without signing up, without providing your real name, email, phone number, or social credentials.
  • Local Storage: Your drawn Tarot cards, personal reflections, notes, and reading journals are stored locally on your device's private database. We do not maintain any central repository of your private readings.

1.2 Information We Process

A. Questions and Tarot Interpretations (Ephemeral Processing)

When you ask a question for an AI-assisted Tarot spread, your prompt and the drawn card identities are transmitted via an encrypted TLS/HTTPS connection to our secure server proxy.

  • This data is processed ephemerally in memory solely to synthesize the Oracle reading in real time and is not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Your questions are never sold, rented, or used to build personalized advertising profiles.

B. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases (Apple App Store & Google Play)

Mystic Tarot offers optional auto-renewable subscription tiers (Tarot Basic and Tarot Pro) to expand reading limits:

  • Payment Security: All financial transactions, billing instruments, and payment details are handled exclusively by Apple In-App Purchase (Apple Inc.) on iOS and Google Play Billing (Google LLC) on Android. We never receive, process, or store credit card numbers or banking data.
  • Cryptographic Store Tokens: Upon purchase, Apple App Store or Google Play provides an anonymous transaction receipt or token (purchaseToken). Our server validates this token directly with Apple or Google developer APIs solely to verify your subscription tier and credit reading quotas.

C. Anonymous Device Identifier & Fair-Usage Limiting

  • To enforce reading quotas (including the 5 lifetime free trial readings) and protect backend servers against automated spam attacks, the app derives an anonymous, one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hash from ANDROID_ID on Android or a random installation UUID on iOS.
  • This identifier is used exclusively to enforce reading quotas and verify subscription entitlements. It is never used for advertising or cross-app tracking, is never shared with third parties, and cannot identify you personally.

D. Technical Logs & Diagnostics

Anonymous, aggregated crash logs, operating system versions, and performance diagnostics may be collected via Apple App Store and Google Play Services to identify technical bugs and enhance stability.

1.3 Third-Party Services

We work with trusted industry providers for infrastructure and billing:

  • Apple App Store & In-App Purchase (Apple Inc.) — For iOS distribution and secure subscription billing.
  • Google Play Billing & Services (Google LLC) — For Android distribution and secure subscription checkout.
  • Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.) — For encrypted routing, API proxying, and anti-DDoS security.
  • AI Interpretation Engine — Cloud-based language processing service used solely to generate real-time textual interpretations based on classical Tarot archetypes.

1.4 Data Retention, Deletion, and User Rights

  • Immediate Local Deletion: You can wipe all reading journals and history inside the app at any time, or permanently remove all local data by clearing application storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Data Requests: You may reach out to us at baka3k@gmail.com for any inquiries regarding server-side quota records, subscription records, or privacy questions.

1.5 Children’s Privacy

Mystic Tarot is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Cancellation Instructions

You may manage or cancel your subscription at any time without penalty:

On iOS (Apple App Store):

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top > Subscriptions.
  3. Select Mystic Tarot and tap Cancel Subscription.

On Android (Google Play):

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile icon at the top right > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Mystic Tarot and select Cancel subscription.
  4. Or visit Google's online portal: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Following cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of the paid billing period.

Restoring Purchases & Refunds

  • Restore: If you switch devices or reinstall the app, tap "Restore Purchases" in the Paywall or Settings to restore your tier.
  • Refunds: All refund requests are governed by Apple App Store or Google Play’s official refund policies. For iOS purchases, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Android purchases, submit requests via your Google Play order history.
