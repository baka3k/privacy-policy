Part 1: Privacy Policy

1.1 Core Principles: Guest-First & Privacy by Design

Privacy by Design: Mystic Tarot does not require account creation, logins, or personal profile data. All reading histories remain strictly stored on your own device.
  • No Account Required: You can explore Tarot spreads immediately without signing up, without providing your real name, email, phone number, or social credentials.
  • Local Storage: Your drawn Tarot cards, personal reflections, notes, and reading journals are stored locally on your device's private database. We do not maintain any central repository of your private readings.

1.2 Information We Process

A. Questions and Tarot Interpretations (Ephemeral Processing)

When you ask a question for an AI-assisted Tarot spread, the prompt and the drawn card identities are transmitted via an encrypted TLS/HTTPS connection to our secure server proxy.

  • This data is processed ephemerally in memory solely to synthesize the Oracle reading in real time.
  • Your questions are never sold, rented, or used to build personalized advertising profiles.

B. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Mystic Tarot offers optional subscription tiers (Basic and Pro) to expand reading limits:

  • Payment Security: All financial transactions, billing instruments, and payment details are handled exclusively by Google Play (Google LLC). We never receive, process, or store credit card numbers or banking data.
  • Cryptographic Store Tokens: Google Play provides an anonymous transaction token (purchaseToken) upon purchase. Our server validates this token with Google Play Developer APIs solely to verify your subscription tier and credit reading quotas.

C. Anonymous Installation ID & Fair-Usage Limiting

  • To enforce monthly limits and protect backend servers against automated spam attacks, the app generates an anonymous random installation identifier (X-Install-Id) kept on your device.
  • This identifier carries no personal information and cannot identify you personally.

D. Technical Logs & Diagnostics

Anonymous crash logs, operating system versions, and performance diagnostics may be collected via Google Play Services to identify technical bugs and enhance stability.

1.3 Third-Party Services

We work with trusted industry providers for infrastructure and billing:

  • Google Play Billing & Services (Google LLC) — For app distribution and secure subscription checkout.
  • Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.) — For encrypted routing, API proxying, and anti-DDoS security.
  • Google Gemini AI (Google LLC) — For processing AI-assisted textual interpretations based on classical Tarot archetypes.

1.4 Data Retention, Deletion, and User Rights

  • Immediate Local Deletion: You can wipe all reading journals and history at any time simply by clearing application data or uninstalling the app.
  • Data Requests: You may reach out to us at baka3k@gmail.com for any inquiries regarding subscription records or privacy questions.

1.5 Children’s Privacy

Mystic Tarot is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
