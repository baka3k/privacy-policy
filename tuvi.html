Part 1: Privacy Policy

1.1 Core Principles: Guest-First & Privacy by Design

Huyền Cơ Mệnh Lý is built with a guest-first, local-first, privacy-by-design architecture:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal details such as your phone number, email address, or social media profile to explore astrological charts and use our core features.
  • On-Device Data Storage: All your saved astrological profiles (Hồ sơ lá số), birth charts, personal reflections, and questions are stored locally on your device via secure on-device storage. We do not store or synchronize your private charts on any central database.

1.2 Information We Process

A. Astrological Input Data & Ephemeral Calculations

When you generate an astrological chart (Tử Vi Đẩu Số, Bát Tự Tứ Trụ, Kinh Dịch Quẻ Mệnh, Chọn Ngày Lành) or request an in-depth reading, the required parameters (solar/lunar birth date, birth hour, gender, and query context) are transmitted over an encrypted TLS connection (HTTPS) to our computational engine and language processing service:

  • This data is used strictly in real-time to compute astronomical positions, stems and branches (Can Chi), palace arrangements, and generate the textual interpretation.
  • Birth data and reading queries are ephemeral and are never sold to data brokers, never shared with unauthorized third parties, and never used for advertising profiling.

B. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

Huyền Cơ Mệnh Lý offers optional subscription tiers (Basic and Pro) to unlock higher monthly consultation limits:

  • Payment Security: All financial transactions, billing instruments, and payment details are handled exclusively and securely by Google Play Billing (Google LLC) on Android and Apple App Store In-App Purchase (Apple Inc.) on iOS.
  • No Financial Data Collection: We never receive, process, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank account details.
  • Cryptographic Store Tokens: When you purchase or restore a subscription, the store platform provides an anonymous cryptographic token (purchaseToken or receipt data). Our backend validates this token directly with official developer APIs solely to activate your plan and allocate consultation quotas.

C. Anonymous Installation ID & Fair-Usage Limiting

  • To enforce monthly quota limits (Free: 15 consultations/month, Basic: 50 consultations/month, Pro: 300 consultations/month) and protect backend servers against automated spam attacks, the app generates an anonymous random installation identifier (X-Install-Id) kept on your device.
  • This identifier contains no personal information and cannot identify you personally.

D. Minimal Device Permissions

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to connect to our secure server for solar-lunar calendar synchronization, generating in-depth interpretations, and verifying subscription receipts.
  • Photo / Storage Access: Requested strictly when you choose to export your chart image or PDF report to your device. We do not inspect, collect, or access your other photos or files.

E. Technical Logs & Diagnostics

Anonymous crash logs, operating system versions, and performance diagnostics may be collected via standard store developer services to help us identify technical bugs and enhance stability.
