1. Privacy Policy

Thank you for choosing PMP Exam Prep: Practice Tests (the "App"), developed by Baka Team. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of data privacy and transparency.

Privacy-First Guarantee: PMP Exam Prep is an offline-first educational application. We do NOT collect, harvest, track, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers.

1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media logins.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use the App.
  • No Advertising / Tracking: The App contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app behavioral tracking mechanisms.
  • No Biometric or Location Data: The App does not access GPS, location services, microphones, cameras, or contact lists.

1.2 Local On-Device Storage

All of your study materials, practice exam attempts, bookmarks, custom notes, accuracy rates, and spaced-repetition flashcard statistics are stored strictly on your device using a local encrypted database (SQLCipher / SQLite).

Because all progress is stored locally, deleting the application will delete your locally saved quiz history unless backed up via standard operating system backups (such as Apple iCloud Backup or Google Drive Device Backup).

1.3 App Store & Platform Diagnostic Data

When you purchase or download the app via the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, transaction and payment details are processed directly and securely by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies. We do not receive or hold any credit card or billing numbers.

Anonymous crash logs and operating system diagnostics may be aggregated by Apple TestFlight / App Store Analytics or Google Play Console to identify runtime crashes and maintain stability. This information is strictly technical and does not identify individual users.

1.4 Children's Privacy

The App is an educational study tool designed for adult professionals preparing for the Project Management Professional (PMP)® certification exam. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone/iPad or Android device?
A: Simply log in with the same Apple ID or Google account used to make the purchase, and download the App from the App Store or Google Play. The full application will automatically be unlocked.

Q: Can I use the app completely offline?
A: Yes! All 1,000+ questions, explanations, formulas, and flashcards are bundled locally inside the App. You can study on flights, subways, or in areas without internet access.

Q: I noticed a typo or disagree with an explanation. How do I report it?
A: Please email us at baka3k@gmail.com with the Question ID and your feedback. Our content team reviews every submission against PMBOK® Guide 7th Edition and Agile Practice Guide.

Q: How quickly will support respond?
A: We typically respond to all support emails within 24 to 48 business hours.
