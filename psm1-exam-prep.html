1. Privacy Policy

Thank you for choosing Scrum Master: PSM Exam Prep (com.baka3k.learn.sm, the "App"), developed by Baka Team. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of data privacy and transparency.

Privacy-First Guarantee: Scrum Master is a 100% offline-first educational application. We do NOT collect, harvest, track, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers.

1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media logins.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use the App.
  • Zero Third-Party Ads or Trackers: The App contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app behavioral tracking mechanisms.
  • No Biometric, Location, or Sensor Data: The App does not access GPS, location services, microphones, cameras, or contact lists.
  • Offline by Design: The App does not require internet access or request network permissions to function.

1.2 Local On-Device Storage & Security

All of your study progress, practice exam attempts, bookmarks, accuracy rates, and spaced-repetition (SM-2) flashcard statistics are stored strictly on your device inside an AES-256 encrypted SQLCipher / SQLite database.

  • On Android, automatic OS cloud backup (allowBackup) is disabled to keep your local database strictly on your device.
  • Deleting the application removes your local study database and preferences from your device.
  • During an active Practice Exam session, screen-capture privacy protection (FLAG_SECURE on Android and an iOS privacy blur shield) is active to simulate authentic exam conditions and protect app-switcher snapshots.

1.3 iOS Privacy Manifest & Platform Processing

In accordance with Apple App Store requirements (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), the App declares zero tracking (NSPrivacyTracking = false) and zero collected data types. It accesses only standard system APIs required for local operation: UserDefaults (CA92.1) for local preferences and FileTimestamp (C617.1) for local database file management.

When you purchase or download the App via the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, transaction and payment details are processed directly and securely by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies. We do not receive or hold any credit card or billing numbers. Anonymous, aggregated crash logs and OS diagnostics may be provided by Apple App Store / TestFlight or Google Play Console if enabled in your device settings.

1.4 Children's Privacy

The App is an educational study tool designed for professionals preparing for Scrum Master certification exams. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
