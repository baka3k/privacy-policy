Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

PMP Exam Prep is a one-time purchase linked to your Apple ID (iOS) or Google Account (Android). If you reinstall the app or change devices, simply download it again using the same account. Your lifetime license will automatically be recognized.

Can I practice offline?

Yes. The entire question bank, detailed explanations, and flashcard system are stored locally in the app on your device. An internet connection is not required to practice or take simulated exams.

How do I report a question error or explanation discrepancy?

Please send an email to baka3k@gmail.com with the Question ID, the current answer, and your suggested correction or rationale. Our content specialists evaluate all question feedback against the latest PMI® ECO standard.

How do I request a refund?

Because payments are handled directly by Apple and Google, developers cannot process refunds directly. For iOS purchases, please visit Apple's official refund portal at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Android, refer to the Google Play Store order history.
